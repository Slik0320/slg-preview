How we work

Three ways we can work together.

Which one fits depends on one question: does your venue hold a liquor licence, and does your event stay inside it? Tell us the venue — we'll tell you the answer.

Option 01

Full Bar Service

For venues without a liquor licence — grounds, halls, sports fields, farms, warehouses, marquees, private venues and activations.

We take the bar end to end. Sam's lodges the trading permit application for your venue and your dates, supplies and manages the stock, provides the equipment and the staff, runs the point-of-sale, and hands you a full reconciliation at the end of the night. All permits are subject to approval by the relevant liquor authority.

Best for: festivals, concerts, sports days, street events, launches and any event on ground that has never been licensed.
Option 02

Managed Bar Partnership

For licensed venues — hotels, conference centres, clubs and established event venues.

Your venue keeps its licence and its trade. We come in behind it and run the operation: stock, equipment, trained staff, supervision and reporting, with our appointed responsible person over the bar. The venue keeps control; you get a professionally run bar and someone else carrying the operational load.

Best for: venues that hold a licence but don't want to run a large event bar themselves — and organisers who want one standard of service wherever they book.
Option 03

Overflow & Outdoor Bars

For events that spill past the venue's licensed area — gardens, lawns, marquees, parking areas, VIP zones and overflow sections.

A venue's licence covers the area on its licence, not the whole property. The moment your crowd moves outside that line, the house bar can't serve them — and the queue moves indoors. We apply to licence and then run the outside areas so the whole site trades, subject to approval by the relevant liquor authority.

Best for: marquee events, garden functions, festivals on venue grounds, and any event where the house bar can't reach the crowd.
