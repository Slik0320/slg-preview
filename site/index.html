Who we are

Three divisions, one township-built business.

Sam's Liquor is a Second-generation, family-owned liquor retail, wholesale and distribution business that has served South African township communities for over 50 years.

What began as a single outlet has grown into an integrated operation spanning retail stores, wholesale trade and a partner distribution centre for major beverage suppliers — combining logistics, warehousing and community presence under one roof.

"Born Ekasi. Built for Mzansi."

Our mission is to become South Africa's leading township retail and wholesale beverage company — combining affordability, technology, logistics and community investment.

  • Inventory management

04
Event Partners

Event Partner

We don't just supply the bar — we run it. Sam's arrives with the licence, the stock, the fridges, the point-of-sale and trained staff, operates the bar for the duration of your event, and hands you a signed reconciliation at the end of the night. You focus on the event.

Stock is supplied on consignment, so there's nothing to buy up front and no deposit to put down. What doesn't sell comes back to us.

  • Licensed, staffed and stocked bar operations
  • No stock purchase, no deposit, no write-offs
  • Fridges, bar units, glassware and POS included
  • Permits and compliance handled by us
